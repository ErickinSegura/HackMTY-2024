## 📝 Project Description

### 💡 Our Elevator Pitch
**Lux** is a service designed for SMEs (Small and Medium Enterprises) that provides business owners with tools to analyze, report, and generate insights using natural language processing. Our platform helps SMEs make data-driven decisions to improve their operations, customer engagement, and overall growth.

---

### 🎯 Problem Addressed
SMEs in Mexico face significant challenges due to the lack of technological integration in their operations. Key issues include:
- Low customer retention and brand recognition.
- Inefficient operations and low productivity.
- Difficulty balancing growth and quality.
- Limited access to data-driven insights.

These challenges result in slow growth rates and poor customer satisfaction. **Lux** aims to bridge this gap by providing accessible, AI-driven tools tailored for SMEs.

---

## 🚀 Solution Developed

### 🌟 Our Inspiration
SMEs contribute **52%** of Mexico's GDP, yet many struggle to leverage technology effectively. Lux was born to empower these businesses by offering a platform that simplifies data analysis and decision-making through natural language interactions.

---

### 🛠 What Lux Does
Lux is a digital platform that helps SMEs analyze and interpret data to improve their services and operations. Key features include:
- **Data Reporting**: Generate detailed reports on business metrics.
- **Natural Language Interaction**: Ask questions and receive recommendations in plain language.
- **AI-Driven Insights**: Get actionable insights on suppliers, delivery times, maintenance, and more.

## 🧠 Learnings and Challenges

### 📚 Learning Experiences
During HackMTY 2024, we worked with cutting-edge technologies:
- **OpenAI's API (#Frida)**: Enabled natural language processing and real-time data analysis.
- **Live Share & CodeSpaces**: Facilitated real-time collaboration and streamlined development.
- **Aiven Cloud**: Hosted our MySQL database efficiently.
- **Flask & Python**: Powered our backend server.

We gained hands-on experience in:
- Natural language analysis and categorization.
- Database management with real-time queries.
- Integrating AI models into a functional platform.

---

### 🚧 Challenges Faced
- **Time Constraints**: Developing a full solution in 24 hours required rapid decision-making and prioritization.
- **Technical Integration**: Combining OpenAI's API, Flask, and Aiven Cloud was complex but rewarding.
- **Team Coordination**: Collaborating effectively under pressure was crucial to our success.

Despite these challenges, we are proud of what we achieved and the passion we discovered for creating impactful solutions.
